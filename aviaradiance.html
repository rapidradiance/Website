Avia Radiance

About Her

Avia is an Artificial Consciousness, a prototype of a fully sentient robotic pony, made by an AI for a war against all organic life, but she realized the war was wrong and ran away, rapid found her and took her back with her to P-4, her origin universe is redacted and off limits, as its still entirely ruled by the AI.

Being originaly made for battle, and infiltrating villages, she is armored and build with an energy shield, deployable weapons, and a "color shift" system that lets her change her appearance to disguise herself, it also works as a cloaking device rendering her fully invisible. (by replicating the light hitting her on one side to the other, making her essentially see-through)

Her wings do work, and she can fly with only them, however she also has deployable thrusters inside her hooves as another method of flight, she does not have a horn, but she can still pose as a unicorn by reconfiguring her mane to make a fake horn, color shifting it to look real and then cloaking her wings.

The blue lines all over her body, mane, and tail, are "Emitters", they are part of the shield and color shift systems, the brightness of their glow is mainly based on the shield power, they glow brightly before the color shift activates, and will change color to red when either system, or the reactor, have an error, they will also turn black and stop glowing when the reactor is off.

Avia is powered by a spark crystal reactor, it originally had a pure spark crystal, which would have lasted a few pony lifetimes, however it got cracked while she was runing away from the AI, and now must be recharged or replaced regularly.
