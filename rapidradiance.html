Rapid Radiance

About Her

Rapid is a genetically engineered pseudo-Alicorn created by the unicorns of unihorn, she is smaller than the average pony and is physically weaker, however her magic, while not being as powerful as an alicorn's, is more powerful then a normal unicorn.

She has the ability to travel between universes without an "Anti-Spark Multi-Universal Matter Transmission Array" by use of an old crystal matrix created by the unihorn group before it disbanded, however it is limited to only one or 2 ponies at a time and most creatures will not survive going through it as it does not have the protections that later methods of multi-verse travel have.

Rapid was found on a doorstep as a foal, the unihorn group had left her there when they disbanded, it is assumed they disbanded because of conflicting opinions about the genetic experimentation that led to rapids creation, but that is not confirmed.

She found the old unihorn lab while exploring the forest one day, it had been dormant inside unihorn mountain until she had gotten close and triggered a saved program, which had been left for her before the place was abandoned, some time after that she and avia founded rising shield, using the old unihorn labs as the HQ until a new HQ was eventually built, before that she was a doctor.

She has high functioning autism, which may be a result of the way she was created, but that is unknown
